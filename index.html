Engineering practice grounded in site reality

Bengal Trust Build Ltd. is a professional construction and engineering company serving residential, commercial and public infrastructure clients. We combine in-house structural and civil design with disciplined site execution, so what is drawn is what gets built.

Our work is guided by three commitments: quality that can be inspected, innovation that lowers lifecycle cost, and development that respects the soil, water and climate conditions of Bangladesh.

Mission

To deliver safe, durable and cost-conscious structures and utility systems through transparent engineering and accountable project management.

Vision

To be the most trusted engineering and construction partner in Bangladesh, known for work that performs long after handover.
